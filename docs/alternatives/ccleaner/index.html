PC Workman vs CCleaner: cleaning without the risky parts

CCleaner is the best-known cleaner on Windows and supports a long list of programs. PC Workman's Cleanup cleans fewer places on purpose, shows every file first, never touches the registry, and adds what CCleaner does not: what takes the rest of your disk and how your free space changes over time.

Where CCleaner is better

  • A long list of supported apps and custom clean options, built over many years.
  • Extras in the paid version: a performance optimizer that puts apps to sleep, a software updater, a driver updater, scheduled cleaning.
  • Privacy wiping of browser history and cookies if you want it.

Where PC Workman is better

  • No registry cleaning. Microsoft does not support registry cleaners (KB2563254); leftover entries take almost no space.
  • Every file shown first, open programs skipped, nothing from the last 24 hours, your own files never ticked for you.
  • What takes your disk: a read-only analysis that says what each big folder is and what to do.
  • Free space over time with a trend and a forecast, and a receipt after every cleanup.
  • Trust: open code, no bundled offers. In 2020 Microsoft Defender began flagging CCleaner installers as potentially unwanted because of bundled offers (BleepingComputer), and in 2025 paid users on CCleaner's own forum complained about version 7 (forum thread).

Who should use which

CCleaner if you want one tool that also wipes privacy traces and updates software, and you are fine with its paid tiers. PC Workman if you want space back without risk, a clear picture of your disk and a cleaner you can read the code of.

Moving from CCleaner

Run PC Workman's scan once: it shows what CCleaner would usually clear (temp files, browser caches, crash dumps) and what it never looks at (Steam shaders per game, driver installers, what takes the rest of the disk). See Cleanup.

Questions

Is there a safe free alternative to CCleaner?

PC Workman's Cleanup: 28 known places, every file shown first, no registry cleaning, open source and free.

Is CCleaner's registry cleaner safe?

Microsoft does not support registry cleaners and warns they can cause problems. The space they free is negligible, so PC Workman does not include one.

Does PC Workman have scheduled cleaning?

No, on purpose. It reminds you when the drive fills up and lets you decide.
