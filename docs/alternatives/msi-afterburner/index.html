PC Workman vs MSI Afterburner: tune the card, or understand the PC

MSI Afterburner is the standard tool for overclocking a graphics card and, with RivaTuner, for in-game overlays. PC Workman does not overclock. It tells you what limits your clocks today, shows an overlay in three presets, and keeps the history Afterburner leaves in log files.

Where MSI Afterburner is better

  • Overclocking and undervolting of the graphics card, with an OC Scanner on NVIDIA.
  • Fan curves for the graphics card on desktops.
  • The RivaTuner overlay: hooks into the game, works in exclusive full screen, frame times and lows.
  • Logging any monitored value to a file.

Where PC Workman is better

  • No risk: Headroom Lab is read only and says whether heat or power limits you before you touch a slider.
  • An overlay without two programs: Default, Compact or Pro, any card; RTSS only for the FPS number.
  • History without a log viewer: months of CPU, GPU and RAM data with the process behind every spike. Afterburner's graphs are the current session; longer history means a log file and another program.
  • The whole PC, not only the graphics card: CPU, memory, drives, startup, cleanup.
  • Laptops: custom fan curves do not work on most laptops in Afterburner (guide); PC Workman's Fan Dashboard says so plainly and plans curves instead.

Who should use which

Afterburner to overclock, undervolt or set a graphics card fan curve, and for the most detailed overlay. PC Workman to see what limits the PC, keep the history and get an overlay without setting up two programs.

Using both

The best of both: Afterburner with RTSS running, PC Workman reading FPS from RTSS for its overlay and history, and Headroom Lab telling you whether the next overclock will hit heat or power. See Game overlay and Headroom Lab.

Questions

Can I get an FPS overlay without MSI Afterburner?

PC Workman's overlay shows load and temperatures without Afterburner; the FPS number itself comes from RivaTuner Statistics Server when it runs.

Does PC Workman overclock my graphics card?

No. It never writes clocks, voltages or power limits. Headroom Lab measures what limits you.

Why does Afterburner not change my laptop fans?

Most laptops keep the fan curve in the BIOS. That is a limit of the laptop, not of Afterburner or PC Workman.

How to open it

Keep Afterburner for tuning and add PC Workman for history, the PC as a whole and a calmer overlay.
