And since you are here: at night I write my own software.

My name is Marcin. By day I drive people around Radom, in Poland. In the evening I sit down at a laptop and build things nobody asked me for.

It started in the Netherlands, in June 2025. I was working twelve-hour shifts in a warehouse, coming back to my room and writing a program on an old laptop, meant to watch over a computer and stop it slowing down for no reason. I did it because I wanted to know how. There was no plan, no company, and nobody waiting for it.

A year later that same program passed review and went into the Microsoft Store. The same file that was written at night, between shifts.

There was a gap in the code. For three weeks I was driving long-haul across Europe with nothing on me but a phone. I did not stop planning or writing, I just did it from a cab instead of a desk. The change history does not show that part.

Nothing much has changed since then except the scale. There is work in the day and code at night. I did try selling once, I even had a small online shop, but marketing patter turned out not to be for me. I would rather build a tool that genuinely helps somebody and give it away.

So I am not selling you anything here. Below you can simply see what came of it.
