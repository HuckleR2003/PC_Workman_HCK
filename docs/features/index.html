PC Workman · Features

Everything PC Workman does

PC Workman is a free system monitor and optimizer for Windows 10 and 11. It reads your sensors and explains them, remembers how your PC behaved for months, cleans and tunes it with a measurement before and after, and answers questions about it offline. Twenty-one features, one program.

21features in one program
648Windows processes described
313games it recognises
0accounts, ads or cloud needed
Built in public since 2025, and mature. Almost every feature on this page works in full. The one held back on purpose is the Fan Dashboard: it plans fan curves and shows measured fan data, but does not drive the fans yet. Stability and tests that cover everything come first.

Monitoring that explains itself

DeepMonitor

Every sensor of the CPU, GPU, RAM, disks, fans and voltages, with the reading explained: which CPU temperature is the real one, what is normal for this PC, and which motherboard sensor reports a false 110 °C.

Instead of HWiNFO, HWMonitor, AIDA64

Activity Memory

CPU, GPU and RAM history kept for more than a year. Go back to yesterday 21:00 and see which process slowed the PC, or compare this month with half a year ago, before a Windows update.

No equivalent in other monitors

Monitoring & Learning

Learns what is normal for this PC under idle, light and heavy load, so 82 °C is judged against your own norm, not a number from a forum. Voltages are watched for drift.

No equivalent in other monitors

PC Map

Your components drawn as a desktop or a laptop, coloured by the temperature now or the 7-day average. Hover a part for its details, click it to open its history.

Instead of an AIDA64 report

Learn more →

Health Report

One HTML file that says whether the PC is healthy: temperatures against the norm, disks, drivers, startup and what to do first. Save it or send it to whoever fixes your PC.

Cleanup and optimization, measured

Cleanup

28 known places in Windows, browsers, games and apps, a disk analysis that says what every big folder is, and free space history with a forecast. Files shown first, open programs skipped, no registry cleaning.

Instead of CCleaner, Disk Cleanup, Storage Sense

Optimization Guide

Six steps from startup to services, each with a measurement before and after and a receipt of what changed, so you know which step helped and can undo it.

Startup Manager

Registry, the Startup folder, Task Scheduler and Store apps in one list, with the boot impact and a recommendation per entry. Disables the way Task Manager does, so it can always be undone.
