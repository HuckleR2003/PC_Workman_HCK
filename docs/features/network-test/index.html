Why is my internet slow? A diagnosis, not just a number

Speedtest gives you a number. The network test in hck_GPT checks your router, the DNS, ping, jitter and packet loss, and says where the problem is: your Wi-Fi, the DNS, or somewhere past your router. A speed test runs only when you say yes.

What it checks

  • Your router (the default gateway): reachable, how fast, how steady. A slow or lossy router means Wi-Fi or the router itself.
  • The DNS: whether names resolve and how long it takes. Pages that load slowly at the start but then run fine are often DNS.
  • The internet past the router: ping, jitter and packet loss to well-known hosts.
  • The adapter: Wi-Fi or cable, and its link speed.

A verdict in plain words

The answer is one of ten verdicts, for example "It is the DNS: the router answers fast, names resolve slowly" or "The problem is past your router: your network is fine, call your provider", with the steps that fit it. Every number in the answer is marked with where it came from, so nothing is guessed.

Only at the end the guide asks whether to measure the speed. On "yes" it downloads a test file from Cloudflare; on "no" nothing more leaves the PC.

Questions

Why is my internet slow on one PC only?

Usually Wi-Fi: weak signal, a busy channel or an old adapter. If the router answers slowly or loses packets from this PC only, the test says it is on your side, not your provider's.

How do I know if it is my router or my provider?

If the router answers fast and steady but hosts past it are slow or lose packets, the problem is past your router. The test says exactly that.

Is the speed test required?

No. It runs only if you say yes at the end of the guide.
