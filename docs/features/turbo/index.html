Feature · TURBO

One switch for gaming, and everything comes back when you stop

TURBO starts four things for a game: RAM Flush when memory passes 75%, a Turbo power plan that follows the game, paused background services, and sleep for idle background apps. Games, anti-cheat, drivers and security tools are never on its lists, and every change is undone when TURBO ends.

What TURBO does

RAM Flush

Above 75% RAM, asks idle programs to return working memory. Only on sustained pressure, not every second.

Turbo power plan

Switches to a high-performance plan while a game or full-screen app runs, keeps the battery plan on a laptop off the charger, restores your plan on exit.

Paused services

Stops the services of the chosen profile (Gaming: 18) and starts them again afterwards.

Sleeping apps

Pauses known background apps idle for 5 minutes (browsers, Discord, Spotify, Teams, OneDrive, Steam, Epic); they wake after 30 minutes or when TURBO ends.

What it never touches

The only apps TURBO can pause are a fixed list of 13 background programs. Games are not on it, and neither is anti-cheat, so nothing a game or its protection needs is ever frozen mid-match. On top of that, anything matching antivirus, antimalware, firewall, Defender, NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, display, audio or the Windows system folders is protected, along with 35 named Windows and security processes. The lists are open to read.

Measured, not promised

Microsoft's own PC Manager shows why measuring matters: in PCMag's test its Boost cut memory use by about 5% and moved a PCMark 10 score by 9 points (summary). TURBO writes an Optimization Receipt for what it did, with the numbers before and after, so "faster" is something you can check.

Questions

Does TURBO increase FPS?

Usually a little and mostly in the lows: fewer background spikes, fewer stutters. If your graphics card is already at 100%, the game is limited by the card and TURBO cannot change that; the receipt shows it honestly.

Can TURBO get me banned by anti-cheat?

No. TURBO never touches the game process or anti-cheat software: they are not on the list of apps it can pause, and it does not inject anything into games.

What happens when I turn TURBO off?

Paused services start again, sleeping apps wake up, and your own power plan comes back. If PC Workman closes unexpectedly, the power plan is restored on exit as well.
