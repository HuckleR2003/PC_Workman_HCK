Feature · Your profile and data

Your data stays on your PC, and you can see exactly what leaves it

History, learned norms, chats and cleanups live on your own disk. Your profile shows what PC Workman knows and exports it. Anonymous telemetry sends a short fixed list of hardware facts and session length, never your IP address or files, and one switch turns it off.

What stays on your PC

  • The history of CPU, GPU and memory use, and the norms learned from it.
  • hck_GPT conversations, cleanup and optimization receipts, health reports.
  • Your profile: the PC's name, hours with PC Workman, games and hours of gaming, recordings, RAM flushes, disk cleanups.

Export my data on the profile page writes it out, so you can keep it or move it. Delete my data removes it from the PC, after a confirmation that says how many files go.

What anonymous telemetry may send

Telemetry is anonymous, and the server keeps only these fields and drops anything else:

FieldWhat it is
install id, session idRandom identifiers, not linked to you
app version, time, kindWhich version, when, which event
session lengthMinutes PC Workman ran
OS, countryWindows version and the country of the request
CPU, cores, GPU, RAM size and speed, disks, motherboard, screensHardware models, to know what to support

No IP address is stored, no file names, no process lists, no chats. The server code is open to read.

How to turn it off

Settings › Privacy: one switch for telemetry, and one for all network use. With network use off, PC Workman works fully offline: no telemetry, no speed test, nothing sent.

Questions

Does PC Workman collect my data?

Your data stays on your disk. Only anonymous telemetry may leave the PC: a fixed list of hardware models, Windows version, app version and session length, without your IP address. You can turn it off.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign-in.

How do I export or remove my data?

Profile › Export my data writes it out. Profile › Delete my data removes the history, learned norms, notes, settings and hck_GPT’s knowledge at the next start, after you confirm; the program stays.

How to open it

In PC Workman: My PC › Your profile, and Settings › Privacy for the switches.
