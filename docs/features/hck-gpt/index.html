How it understands you

hck_GPT is not a cloud language model. A small classifier trained on thousands of Polish and English phrases (Naive Bayes on words and word pairs, under a millisecond per question) works together with rules for the questions people actually ask: "laguje kiedy gram", "my fans are loud", "co mogę usunąć". It runs on your PC and nothing is sent anywhere.

Answers from your data

  • Temperatures against the norm PC Workman learned for this PC and this load.
  • History: what was heavy yesterday at 21:00, the trend of your free disk space.
  • Processes: what a process is and whether it can go, from the library of 522.
  • Startup and services: what to keep, delay or turn off, and why.

Every answer that points to a fix carries a link straight to the tab: Cleanup, Startup, Services, Upgrade Readiness.

Questions

Does hck_GPT need the internet?

No. It runs on your PC. The only optional network use in its answers is the speed test in the network guide, and it asks before running it.

Is hck_GPT a ChatGPT model?

No. It is PC Workman's own offline assistant: a small local classifier and rules, built for questions about this PC.

What can I ask?

Why the PC is slow, whether a temperature is normal, what a process is, what to remove from startup, what takes the disk space, what to upgrade, why the internet is slow. Polish or English.

How to open it

In PC Workman: the hck_GPT bar at the bottom of every page. Type a question or pick one of the suggestions.
