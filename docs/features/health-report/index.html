Is my PC healthy? One page, one score, what to do first

Health Report reads what Windows and PC Workman know about your PC: unexpected restarts, drive errors, temperatures, memory, the age of the graphics driver and startup. It turns them into a score out of 100, says what to do first, and saves it all as one HTML page.

What the score is made of

PartWeightWhat it looks at

Drives20Free space and the state of each drive

Memory15How full the RAM usually is, and whether it runs at its rated speed (XMP)
Drivers10The age of the graphics driver
Startup10How much starts with Windows

What to do first, in plain words

A score alone helps nobody. Under it the report lists what to do first, worst first, each with a link to the tab that fixes it. On my PC: "Memory: turn on XMP in the BIOS and consider more RAM" and "Stability: restarts without a clean shutdown are usually the power supply, heat or a driver". Then all findings, as plain sentences: "RAM 8 GB is usually 75% full", "RAM runs at 2133 MHz, without XMP some performance is left behind", "4 restarts without a clean shutdown in 30 days".

Save it, compare it, send it

Save the report (HTML) writes one file that opens in any browser, with no PC Workman needed. The next report shows what changed since the last one, part by part. Send the file to whoever helps you with your PC: it says more than "it is slow sometimes". Everything is collected locally; the report says so at the bottom.

Where the others are better. An AIDA64 report lists every detail of every device, which a repair shop sometimes needs. The Health Report is the page you read in a minute and act on.

One score everywhere (1.9.7)

  • One health score behind the report, the Health centre, hck_GPT and Monitoring. Before 1.9.7 there were three, and hck_GPT could say 100/100 on a PC whose RAM hit 99.9% every day.
  • Memory is judged by time under pressure: the share of the last 7 days the RAM spent above 75% and above 90%, not only its average.
  • Snapshots inside the program: save one by hand, on a schedule (every 1 to 30 days, monthly or your own) or automatically after a Windows or graphics driver update, and compare.
  • “Ask hck_GPT” on every point of “What to do first”: step by step, with this PC's numbers, and a link to the page where each step is done.

Questions

How do I check if my PC is healthy?

Open My PC › Health report in PC Workman. It reads the event log, drives, temperatures, memory, drivers and startup and gives a score out of 100 with what to do first.

Why does my PC restart without warning?

Restarts without a clean shutdown are usually the power supply, overheating or a driver. The report counts them over 30 days; the temperature history and the drivers page help find which one.

What is XMP and why does the report mention it?

XMP is the memory profile that runs RAM at its rated speed. With it off, RAM runs at a slower default (2133 MHz on my PC). Turning it on in the BIOS is free performance.
