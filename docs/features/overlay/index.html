FPS and temperatures over your game, nothing you did not choose

The overlay floats over a game and shows only what you pick: CPU, GPU and RAM load, temperatures, clocks, VRAM, the 12 V rail and FPS. Three presets to start, sizes and themes to taste, and it never takes focus away from the game.

What it shows

Default

CPU and GPU load and temperature, RAM, FPS.

Compact

CPU, GPU and RAM load. Three numbers, nothing else.

Pro

Adds the CPU clock, VRAM and the 12 V rail: nine values.

Your own

Pick from CPU %, temperature, clock, power; GPU %, temperature, VRAM, power; RAM % and GB; 12 V; FPS.

Rows per component in their own colour, FPS in a tall box on the right. Sizes S, M and L, themes dark, slate and high contrast, and opacity to taste. A click moves it to the next corner.

Where FPS comes from

When MSI Afterburner or RivaTuner Statistics Server runs, PC Workman reads the frame rate from it. When it does not, the FPS box says "--" instead of guessing. Everything else (load, temperatures, clocks) comes from PC Workman itself, so the overlay works without installing anything else, on NVIDIA, AMD and Intel.

The overlay window never takes focus, so a click in the game stays in the game. It floats over games in borderless or windowed mode.

Where the others are better. RTSS hooks into the game itself, so it works in exclusive full screen and draws a frame time graph. If you tune a game frame by frame, keep it; PC Workman reads its FPS and frame times and shows 1% and 0.1% low under the FPS.

Questions

How do I show FPS and temperatures in a game?

Turn on the overlay in PC Workman's Gaming tile, pick Default, Compact or Pro, and start the game in borderless or windowed mode. For FPS, keep MSI Afterburner or RTSS running.

Does the overlay lower FPS?

Barely: it is a small window that refreshes a few times a second and never hooks into the game.

Why does the FPS box show --?

PC Workman reads FPS from RivaTuner Statistics Server. When RTSS is not running, it shows -- instead of a made-up number.

How to open it

In PC Workman: My PC › Gaming / In-Game, choose a preset and turn the overlay on.
